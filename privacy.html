Privacy Policy

Scheme Kings Analytics. Last updated October 14, 2026.

Scheme Kings Analytics is a private tool used by the owner of the King Reggie and Campus Kings YouTube channels to review analytics for those channels. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the tool asks for read-only access to YouTube channel data and YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources, impressions and similar statistics). It cannot upload, edit or delete anything on YouTube.

How the data is used

The data is used only to build charts and reports about the owner's own channels. It is stored on the owner's own computer and is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Google user data

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The tool also uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions. Deleting the tool's local data folder removes everything it stored.
